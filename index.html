Captain Tips & Tricks

Descent: know the correct level to approach the runway, as Roblox does not use normal plane physics. For immediate descent push the nose down, at -3 AOA max. Senior staff+ may go to -6 AOA for an immediate need to descend. Only pull the nose up when very close and at low speed. WATCH THE STALL SPEED.
Emergency flights: the fire is only visual. Listen to your host at all times and land. Do not go around unless told to. Let the fire trucks approach after you have landed.
Listen to your Deck Manager, Supervisor or Host. They are experienced, and you will never be told off for following their instruction.
If you can't reach the cruise pad because your approach is wrong or you are too low, perform a Go Around and try again.

Captain Priority & Decisions

If you can't be captain, contact the Deck Manager (or the host if there is none) for a valid reason, not because you don't want to.
When a superior gives an instruction, do it immediately. They take responsibility for the order. Fighting back against a superior leads to severe punishments.
Air worthiness: you are responsible for the aircraft and can decide if it needs changing. If you think it is not airworthy during delag, notify the host so the aircraft can be changed or solved by your Deck Manager.

Jetway
Movable bridge connecting terminal to aircraft. Used at: LHR (dep+arr), MNI (dep), PUJ (dep). Reagan and Paine Field NOT in use.
⚠️ Do NOT detach without explicit permission from Flight Host or Ground Manager. Never walk on jetway while moving.
🎮 Controls
W/S/A/D = Movement  ·  Z/X = Extension  ·  Q/E = Rotate Cab Left/Right
R/F = Rotate Cab Up/Down  ·  T/G = Canopy Up/Down  ·  C/V = Camera View
🔌 Attach Procedure
1Get inside the Jetway. Walk to the front and click the prompt to start manipulating it.
2GUI is on the right side: sun icon = lights, 4-boxes icon = doors, last icon = controls menu.
3Before moving: turn on fan, spot lights, and caution lights.
4Move slowly to the door. Adjust height and cab rotation carefully. Change camera for good alignment perspective.
5Once aligned: put canopy down, turn OFF spot lights + caution lights.
6Turn ON all 4 lights: Deck Lights, Rotunda Lights, Cab Lights and Tunnel Lights.
7To detach: reverse all steps. Only with explicit permission from Flight Host or Ground Manager.

Moving: Fan ON, Spot Lights ON, Caution Lights ON
Attached: Deck Lights, Rotunda Lights, Cab Lights, Tunnel Lights ON
Spot Lights + Caution Lights OFF when attached

6Remove red ribbons and open the aircraft door.

Airsteps
Like Airstairs but require a Clark CT30 tug to move. Used on back doors of narrowbody (remote stand) and L1 at smaller airports. Never used on widebody.
⚠️ Always lower airsteps when in transit. Do not use Clark CT30 to position at door or remove from door.

C = Anchor  ·  Z/X = Lower/raise height
Icons on side panel to work with airsteps

1Run :jheight me 17 in the command bar.

🔌 Attach Procedure
1Insert Clark CT30 + Airsteps. Get in tug (hold F), turn on all lights.
2Drive up to Airsteps. Press arrow icon to lower the latch. Pull toward tug until plug icon appears — click it.

6At the door: press icon → Anchor. Remove red ribbon. Pull railings to connect to aircraft. Open door.
7⚠️ Do NOT pull railings inside the aircraft. Ensure railings are not clipped in the door.
8To detach: reverse all steps. When pushing away, do not hit the wings.

Air Conditioning Unit (ACU)
Supplies cool air into aircraft while APU is off. Detach at XX:07–XX:09.
⚠️ This vehicle can fling. If it flings, do not attach it — notify your Ground Manager. Make as FEW turns as possible. Practice on a private server first.
🎮 Controls
B = Beacon  ·  H = Headlights  ·  C = Anchor  ·  V = Anchor the ACU
🔌 Attach Procedure
1Spawn ACU + ACU tug. Get in tug, turn on Beacon and Headlights.
2Align tug with the wedge of the ACU. Hop out, press E at the wedge to connect (look for "Weld ACU GF/30" prompt).
3Drive ACU to the front of the aircraft, Captain side, facing the belly. Make as few turns as possible — it spins out easily.
4Anchor ACU with V. Hop out. Disconnect tug and park it nearby.
5Head to the hoses. Use E + move mouse to belly of aircraft + B to connect the hose.
6Go to side of ACU, find E prompt for Control Panel. Turn on fan, select temperature and fan speed.
7To detach: reverse all steps.
⚠️ ACU Tips
· The ACU can spin out and get stuck VERY easily on turns.
· Use extreme caution on all turns.
· Practice driving on a private server before using in a live flight.
· If it flings, do not try to attach it — let your Ground Manager know immediately.
