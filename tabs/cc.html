Dress Code — R.F.M Standard
Realistic · Friendly · Modest — Ask yourself these three questions when equipping your face.
👩 Female Requirements
· Equip the Woman package (free) before joining the game — bugs if done in-game.
· Hair: natural colors only (black, brown, blonde, ginger). Ombre style is the only exception to dyed hair. Must NOT cover the face.
· Accessories: max 2 accessories. Glasses, sunglasses on head, earrings are all fine. Keep it professional and realistic.
· Faces: Miss Scarlet, Cat Eye, Sapphire Glaze and similar — warm, approachable, realistic.
· You may use :face and :hat commands in-game (no need to purchase).
👨 Male Requirements
· No body packages — unequip everything (blocky avatar). No exceptions.
· Hair: natural colors only (black, brown, blonde, ginger). Must NOT cover the eyes or face. Neat and tidy.
· Accessories: max 2 accessories. Glasses, sunglasses on head only. No earrings.
· Faces: Joyful Smile, Skeptic, Sigmund and similar — professional and welcoming.
· Head: use the default Roblox head — do not equip a catalog head.

🔌 Setup Procedure
1Approach the gate desk.
2Run :to [desk] to enter the desk area.
3Hold E to open the Queue Management System.
4Click Open to open the queue for passengers.
5Assist passengers. Click NEXT after each passenger to remove them from queue.
📋 Important Rules
· Max 3 amenities per passenger. If they ask for more, politely decline.
· Do NOT serve food or drinks at the gate.
· You may give out freecam: :freecam [user]
· Only click each item once to avoid inventory spam.
· Gate agents do NOT do the CC walk — board during economy boarding.
· Close your queue before boarding: announce gate is closed.

Mood Lighting Guide
Mandatory on ALL flights. Only Cabin Managers change lighting — CC may only do so if no CM is present. Abusing the panel = HRD action.
🎛️ Panel Types
Aeroware panel — select color → click "Change".
Vuela panel — set color → click "Set Color".
ZEROtech panel — use Shift to select same color for all sections.

Stars can only be enabled once at cruising altitude.
Panel is usually above jump seats at the L1 door (sometimes L2).
💡 Lighting Schedule
PhaseColor
🛬 BoardingWarm welcoming color (blue/purple/soft tones)
🛫 Takeoff & Landing approachOFF / Black / Dark grey
✈️ CruisingPink (stars optional)
⬇️ DescentGrey / Dark (same as takeoff)
🏁 DeboardingWhite or warm yellow/off-white
📋 When to Change
· Boarding — after CC walk is complete
· Takeoff — right before plane enters runway → OFF/dark
· Cruising — once anchored on cruise pad → pink
· Descent — once aircraft leaves cruise pad → grey/dark
· Deboarding — once plane parks at gate → white/warm yellow
💡 If you notice the CM forgot to change lighting, feel free to remind them! Only change it yourself if no CM is present.
