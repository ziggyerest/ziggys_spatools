Squawk Codes

click to copy
0000, 7777, 7500, 7600: Never to be used. Reserved for military transponders or forbidden emergency roleplays.
click to copy
0001 - 7776: Commercial aviation squawk codes. Can be used by all commercial aircraft during a flight.
click to copy
7700: Emergency only. Alerts ATC that the aircraft has an emergency.
click to copy
Any abuse of codes can lead to consequences. Passengers may know some of these codes.

Standard Taxi Instruction Types

click to copy
Push tail left/right: Sub-instruction for pushback. Decides which side the tail goes.
click to copy
Taxi to: Where and how to taxi. Includes the taxi route the Captain must follow.
click to copy
Hold position (of): Aircraft must stop immediately or at a taxi point. High priority issues only, such as someone standing or something blocking the route.
click to copy
Hold short of: Aircraft will not line up on the runway yet. Only when the host says so, for non-urgent aircraft issues.
click to copy
Line up and wait: Aircraft taxis onto the runway but does not take off.
click to copy
Cleared for takeoff: Clearance for the aircraft to take off.
click to copy
All instructions are in the ATC document. Leaking any part of it is strictly prohibited.
