Jetway
Movable bridge connecting terminal to aircraft. Used at: LHR (dep+arr), MNI (dep), PUJ (dep). Reagan and Paine Field NOT in use.
⚠️ Do NOT detach without explicit permission from Flight Host or Ground Manager. Never walk on jetway while moving.
🎮 Controls
W/S/A/D = Movement  ·  Z/X = Extension  ·  Q/E = Rotate Cab Left/Right
R/F = Rotate Cab Up/Down  ·  T/G = Canopy Up/Down  ·  C/V = Camera View
🔌 Attach Procedure
1Get inside the Jetway. Walk to the front and click the prompt to start manipulating it.
2GUI is on the right side: sun icon = lights, 4-boxes icon = doors, last icon = controls menu.
3Before moving: turn on fan, spot lights, and caution lights.
4Move slowly to the door. Adjust height and cab rotation carefully. Change camera for good alignment perspective.
5Once aligned: put canopy down, turn OFF spot lights + caution lights.
6Turn ON all 4 lights: Deck Lights, Rotunda Lights, Cab Lights and Tunnel Lights.
7To detach: reverse all steps. Only with explicit permission from Flight Host or Ground Manager.
💡 Lights Reference
Moving: Fan ON, Spot Lights ON, Caution Lights ON
Attached: Deck Lights, Rotunda Lights, Cab Lights, Tunnel Lights ON
Spot Lights + Caution Lights OFF when attached

Airstairs
Board passengers from tarmac. 2 versions: Glasscover (L1 door) and Regular (L2 door, widebody only). No Clark CT30 needed — self-propelled.
⚠️ B737 exception: nothing on L1 — inbuilt stairs. Must be completely still when anchoring.
🎮 Controls
Z = Raise  ·  X = Lower  ·  C = Anchor  ·  V = First Person
L = Headlights  ·  K = Spot Lights  ·  J = Stair Lights  ·  P = Caution Lights
Startup button = Start engines  ·  GUI in lower right corner
🔌 Attach Procedure
1Spawn airstairs and jump in. Turn on headlights and caution lights.
2Line up carefully to your door (L1 = Glasscover, L2 = Regular on widebody).
3Raise height to match the door using Z. Slowly drive up to connect.
4When attached with no gap, make sure you are completely still, then anchor (C).
5After anchoring: turn OFF headlights + caution. Turn ON stair lights (J).
6Remove red ribbons and open the aircraft door.

Airsteps
Like Airstairs but require a Clark CT30 tug to move. Used on back doors of narrowbody (remote stand) and L1 at smaller airports. Never used on widebody.
⚠️ Always lower airsteps when in transit. Do not use Clark CT30 to position at door or remove from door.
🎮 Controls (Airsteps)
C = Anchor  ·  Z/X = Lower/raise height
Icons on side panel to work with airsteps
🎮 Controls (Clark CT30 Tug)
F = Get into tug  ·  J = Caution Lights  ·  H = Headlights
📦 Clark CT30 Insert Procedure
1Run :jheight me 17 in the command bar.
2Jump and insert the Clark CT30 Tug at the peak of your jump.
🔌 Attach Procedure
1Insert Clark CT30 + Airsteps. Get in tug (hold F), turn on all lights.
2Drive up to Airsteps. Press arrow icon to lower the latch. Pull toward tug until plug icon appears — click it.
3Drive Airsteps to designated door. Take great care lining up.
4Stop close to door, exit tug, go to side of Airsteps, click the icon. Menu pops up — turn on engine, lower height to match door.
5Disconnect Clark CT30 and park it. Pull Airsteps to the door by hand.
6At the door: press icon → Anchor. Remove red ribbon. Pull railings to connect to aircraft. Open door.
7⚠️ Do NOT pull railings inside the aircraft. Ensure railings are not clipped in the door.
8To detach: reverse all steps. When pushing away, do not hit the wings.
📍 Airsteps same placement table as Airstairs
Narrowbody back doors = always Airsteps on remote stand. L1 = Airsteps at smaller airports (no jetway). Widebody = Airsteps NEVER used. See Airstairs tab for full airport table.

Air Conditioning Unit (ACU)
Supplies cool air into aircraft while APU is off. Detach at XX:07–XX:09.
⚠️ This vehicle can fling. If it flings, do not attach it — notify your Ground Manager. Make as FEW turns as possible. Practice on a private server first.
🎮 Controls
B = Beacon  ·  H = Headlights  ·  C = Anchor  ·  V = Anchor the ACU
🔌 Attach Procedure
1Spawn ACU + ACU tug. Get in tug, turn on Beacon and Headlights.
2Align tug with the wedge of the ACU. Hop out, press E at the wedge to connect (look for "Weld ACU GF/30" prompt).
3Drive ACU to the front of the aircraft, Captain side, facing the belly. Make as few turns as possible — it spins out easily.
4Anchor ACU with V. Hop out. Disconnect tug and park it nearby.
5Head to the hoses. Use E + move mouse to belly of aircraft + B to connect the hose.
6Go to side of ACU, find E prompt for Control Panel. Turn on fan, select temperature and fan speed.
7To detach: reverse all steps.
⚠️ ACU Tips
· The ACU can spin out and get stuck VERY easily on turns.
· Use extreme caution on all turns.
· Practice driving on a private server before using in a live flight.
· If it flings, do not try to attach it — let your Ground Manager know immediately.

Catering Truck
Supplies meals and drinks to aircraft. Attach: R1 door on narrowbody, R1 and R2 on widebody. Detach when all carts unloaded (RP).
⚠️ Will ALWAYS spawn stuck in the air. Ask Ground Manager or host to bring it down. Last resort: :jpower me 200
🎮 Controls (arrow-box icon, bottom left)
H = Headlights  ·  K = Caution Lights  ·  J = Top Lights
Z = Lift Truck  ·  X = Lower Truck  ·  G = Extend Platform  ·  V = Retract Platform
C = Anchor  ·  F = Open Front Door
🔌 Attach Procedure
1Insert catering truck — it spawns in the air. Ask GM or host to bring it down.
2Get in (car icon), start engine, lower elevator as much as possible. Turn on headlights, caution lights, top lights.
3Drive to the aircraft's door (R1 narrowbody, R1+R2 widebody). Line up carefully.
4Raise truck to height of door. Extend platform (G). Anchor (C) when neatly positioned.
5Turn OFF caution lights and headlights. Open front door (F).
6Do NOT use the "unload carts" option. Wait 3–5 minutes, then detach.
🔌 Detach Procedure
1Close aircraft door first.
2Get in truck. Close front door (F). Turn on caution + headlights. Start engine. Unanchor.
3Lower elevator (X). Drive to parking/hangar. Turn off all lights.

Follow Me Car
Guides aircraft to stand after arrival. Arrival airport only. Speed limit: 40 MPH. Match aircraft speed — no sudden moves.
⚠️ Will ALWAYS spawn stuck in the air. Ask GM or host to bring it down. Last resort: :jpower me 200
🎮 Controls (arrow-box icon, bottom left)
H = Headlights  ·  K = Caution Lights  ·  J = Top Lights  ·  Light Patterns = Top Lights mode
X = Strobe Lights  ·  B = Caution Sound  ·  M = Wipers  ·  C = Anchor  ·  F = Fog Light
Startup Button = Engine  ·  Board Console = Follow Me Sign
🔌 Procedure
1Spawn Follow Me Car. Ask GM or host to bring it down.
2When aircraft starts descending: enter car, turn on headlights, head to expected exit. Ask Flight Deck which side they land on.
3When aircraft exits runway: park in front of it. Turn on Caution Lights, Top Lights (Beep-Boop or Flicker pattern), and Follow Me sign (arrow-box icon).
4Guide to stand via shortest route. Stay at aircraft speed. Slow on turns. No sudden speeding/braking.
5Approaching stand: leave space between aircraft and marshaller. Park Follow Me Car, turn off ALL lights including Follow Me sign.

Shuttle Bus & Irizar Bus
Shuttle Bus: moves passengers across airport (dep+arr). Irizar Bus: transports passengers to resort after arrival. Both have 40 MPH speed limit.
⚠️ Do NOT leave either bus while anchored — it breaks the vehicle. Set to neutral gear first.
🚌 Shuttle Bus Procedure
1Insert Shuttle Bus. Turn on Lights and Interior Lights. Select gear and drive toward passengers (ask GM/host where to go).
2At boarding spot: turn off lights, anchor, open all doors on one side. Leave Interior Lights ON.
3When host says depart: close doors, enable seat lock, unanchor, turn on lights, drive to aircraft.
4At aircraft: anchor, open doors, disable seat lock. Wait for all passengers to disembark.
5If returning for more passengers: repeat. Otherwise park in designated spot.
🚌 Irizar Bus Procedure
1GM or host spawns the Irizar Bus. If no GM and host unavailable: insert Irizar LWB Bus yourself, park, open door, set gear to neutral.
2Come back after completing other arrival duties. Get in and anchor.
3When host allows: close doors, unanchor, turn on lights. Drive to resort (40 MPH max).
4At resort: park, turn off lights, anchor, open doors.
💡 Shuttle Bus controls: Q/E = Gears. Irizar Bus GUI shown on screen when in vehicle.

Baggage Loader & Baggage Truck
Baggage Loader: loads bags onto aircraft. Baggage Truck (Clark CT30 + 2 carts): transports bags to/from loader. Both detach after investor boarding.
🎮 Baggage Loader Controls
V = First Person  ·  X/Z = Increase/Decrease Height  ·  C = Anchor
K = Spot Lights  ·  J = Caution Lights  ·  H = Headlights
🔌 Baggage Loader — Attach
1Insert Baggage Loader. Turn on Headlights and Caution Lights. Drive to aircraft.
2Stop near aircraft and open baggage hold door. Run /bag in chat.
3Baggage system pops up when possible to attach. Proceed to attach loader.
4Once attached: turn OFF headlights, turn ON Spot Lights.
📦 Practical Baggage Loading
1Run /bag. Click on a bag — outline lights up.
2Click a free spot on the Baggage Loader — spot lights up.
3Press "Move" in Baggage GUI. Bag loads automatically.
4To unload: walk up to loader, hold "point" icon that pops up.
🔌 Baggage Truck — Attach
1Run :jheight me 17, jump, insert Clark CT30 at peak of jump. Insert 2 Baggage Carts too.
2Get in Clark CT30 (hold F), turn on all lights. Drive to Baggage Carts.
3Run /bag. Lower latch, pull each cart to tug until plug icon appears — click it for both.
4Roleplay: park next to Baggage Loader. Practical: drive to conveyor belt near hangar.
📦 Practical Baggage Truck Loading
1Drive to conveyor belt. Open cart cover (finger UI near cart).
2Click bag → click free space in cart → press "Move". Repeat.
3Close cover. Drive to Baggage Loader. Load same way after check-in closes (XX:10).
4Unloading: same technique. Drive bags to baggage collection point.

Marshalling
Used at both departure and arrival airports. Command: :give me marshal — select orange color.
📍 Positioning
Stand a respectable distance from the yellow stop line — preferably behind the tarmac piece so the captain can clearly see you. Do not stand too close or the captain may not be able to see your signals.
🚦 Signals
1Aircraft approaching — use Left or Right buttons to guide the aircraft to the centerline.
2Aircraft aligned with centerline — use Forward motion to signal to continue.
3Aircraft getting close — use Slow Forward motion to signal to slow down.
4Aircraft at the stop marker — use Stop motion to signal to stop.
5Aircraft stopped on centerline — use Stop Engines motion to signal to anchor and shut down.

Wingwalking
Used during pushback. Stand under wing tips and nose to ensure wings don't collide with objects. NOT used at Southwest Regional or Okhotsk Monbetsu (unless regular stand).
⚠️ Do NOT change your speed unless wingwalking is actively in progress.
📍 3 Positions
· Left — left wing tip
· Right — right wing tip
· Front — under the nose
⌨️ Keybind Setup
1Type !keybinds in command bar. Press Add.
2Command: :speed me 6 → assign to a numpad key → Save.
3Repeat for all 4 speeds: 6, 10, 16, 26.

3Get marshalling sticks: :give me mar. Set Face Me position + red lights.
4Follow the aircraft during pushback. Match its speed using your keybinds.
5When pushback finishes, walk back to gate and await GM instructions.
