❓ Common Questions
Economy check-in time

What does XX:00 mean
XX:00 displays the minute of time — it just means the minute of any specific hour! For me the time would be [TIME].
Class says no seats left
It says zero seats left because the class isn't open yet — it will open at [TIME]!

Gate Agent
No tools required · Stand at the gate desk · Run :to to enter desk · Hold E to open Queue Management System
Name: Firstname L. [-] Gate Agent [-] [ENG]
⚠️ Gate Agents do NOT do the CC Walk — board once economy boarding starts · Cannot serve food or drinks at gate
👋 Greeting
Standard
Hey! I'm [NAME], your gate agent today. What can I do for you?
Formal
Welcome! I'm [NAME] on the gate today — need anything?
🚪 Gate Setup Steps
1Approach the gate desk
2Run :to command to enter
3Hold E to open Queue Management System
4Click Open to open the queue for passengers
5Assist passengers — click NEXT after each one
🎒 Amenities (max 3 per passenger)
Over limit
Sorry, passengers can only get up to 3 amenities! Is there anything else I can do for you?
Freecam
:freecam [user]
💡 You can give freecam at the gate — you cannot serve food or drinks
🚫 No Ticket
Deny entry
Sorry, I can't let you through without a ticket — head to check-in and they'll get you sorted!
Direct to CSA
Head over to the CSA at check-in — they'll sort you out!
