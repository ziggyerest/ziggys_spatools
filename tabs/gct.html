GCT quota is 3 Quota Points (QP): reading M2 exams (six exams = one point), hosting M3 training (one point per training), M4 flight supervision (one point per supervision), or supervising a GCT trial (one point per supervision).

Module 2 — Theory

Part A covers the department as a whole, Part B is an ATC exam. Pass mark is 85%. The bot gives a suggested answer per question — don't rely on it 100%, especially for open-ended questions. If the trainee has submitted a 4th attempt, only GCL may read it.
AI exams: never fail a trainee yourself for suspected AI use — report to HRD in the Pacific Academy and they'll instruct you. Signs (several together, not one alone): answers don't fit SPA context (read as real life), overly polished writing, repeated sentence structure, perfect formal grammar with no personality.

Module 3 — Practical

Split into Part A and Part B. Schedule via /training_schedule and attach a thread so trainees can register.

Module 4 — In-flight supervision

Get the host's permission before supervising. Alliance, private and similar flight types automatically prohibit M4s. Announce your supervision in #upcoming-sv using the required format.
Mark with /gct_mark_m4 once the flight ends. Log and mark all trainees in ONE command, separated by commas (User1, User2, User3). DM each trainee every format and certificate the bot generates — unless marking went to SGM review, in which case it's their responsibility. The Graduation Certificate for M4 passers appears below the bot's message and is visible only to you; it must be sent to the trainee.

Situation handling

Misbehaving trainee: take evidence (screenshots/videos), dismiss from the training (or ask the Flight Host to dismiss if misbehaviour happens mid-flight), then report to HRD in the Pacific Academy.
Ticket handling: don't step into tickets claimed by someone else without permission. Write professionally and grammatically. Debate a ticket in the trainer channels, not the ticket channel. Don't overping roles — once per 24 hours unless necessary. Don't claim a ticket you can't actually answer.

M3 & M4 Marking

To pass a role, the trainee must complete the key step and earn the required share of marks for that role. One failed role is your discretion to pass or fail overall. Two or more failed roles is an automatic session fail. Use /gct_mark_m2 and /gct_mark_m4 (not the legacy commands) until told otherwise.
