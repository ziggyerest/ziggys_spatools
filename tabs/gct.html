Quota
GCT quota: 3 Quota Points (QP), earned via:
  • Reading M2 exams — 6 exams = 1 point
  • Hosting M3 Training — 1 point per training
  • M4 flight supervision — 1 point per supervision
  • Supervising a GCT trial — 1 point per supervision
Check your total via /gct_profile (the GCT database doesn't show QPs for every month you've been a GCT — the command does). Leaderboard via /gct_leaderboard.
Module 2 — Theory Exam
Split into Part A (department as a whole) and Part B (ATC).
  • /review_exam_list — check if any exams are left to read
  • /review_exam — read/mark an exam (bot gives a suggested answer per question — don't rely on it 100%, especially open-ended ones; bot auto-calculates the result, 85% to pass)
  • /review_cancel — cancel a review in progress
  • /review_view — view only, no marking, usable freely
4th attempts may only be read by GCL.
Server Set-up
  1. Join your private server. Recommended airports: Mark Neris, LHR, Punta Cana.
  2. Insert a narrowbody aircraft (one per trainee), anchored near the gates.
  3. Setmessage: :setmessage Welcome to this Ground Crew Module 3 part A Training! [-] Host: Username [-] Time: Time in EDT [-] Good luck!
  4. Equip the standard Ground Manager uniform.
  5. Name yourself: :name me GCXXXX [-] Username [-] Training Host [-] Languages
  6. If over 4 trainees:
    • Request a TA/GCT in #request-ta
    • You may dismiss (not fail) trainees who can't name/uniform themselves in a reasonable time
    • All vehicles except marshalling, jetway and wingwalking can run on mesh planes (put an invisible part under each mesh plane so the lavatory truck hose can attach)
    • Constantly swap trainees on the physical gates — after jetway attach, return it to default position and teleport the next trainee; your TA can assist trainees at the mesh planes meanwhile
Set-up notes:
  • More trainees than jetways (optional): marshalling/jetway on one or two real aircraft, rest of vehicles on mesh planes if the airport has them (e.g. Mark Neris)
  • If vehicles keep despawning: :shutdown the server → change airport → contact GCL. All vehicles must be working during the training.
  • Aeroware (A318/A321) preferred. If not working, other aircraft types are fine, but if it doesn't stay anchored after leaving the CPT seat, anchor manually with btools — doors won't work in that case, so inform the trainee and how to work around it.
Server Opening
  1. In #training-rooms, create a training room. Rename ticket to gc-part-a via /ticket rename, add trainees via /ticket add.
  2. Run !profile on each trainee → Game tab. Accelerometer/gyroscope or no keyboard = likely mobile/tablet → report ticket in academy server with a screenshot, dismiss the trainee.
  3. If not ranked, rank in #trainee-ranking: ;trainee-rank <full username> 120
  4. Check the trainee database — remind them to fill the registration form if missing.
  5. :slock the server, remove the private server link from the training announcement.
  6. Assign all trainees as Ground Crew via the staff panel.
:ann Welcome to this Ground Operations Module 3 part A training! I am NAME, and I will be your trainer during this session.
:ann Grammar is required for this training, including team chat.
:ann You are allowed to use the guide, but your time is limited.
:ann Please equip your uniform and name yourself. Use GC---- as a callsign.
:ann We will now proceed with marshalling. Equip marshall sticks via :give me mar. One-by-one.
Attaching
Vehicles: Fuel truck, Catering truck, Lavatory truck, GPU, ACU, Jetway — ACU attached last (if it flings, you may exempt the trainee if everything else was done correctly beforehand).
Time limit: 25 minutes. Chat disabled → exempt from GPU, time limit −3 min. Fails to finish in time → fail immediately.
:notify all Jetway, Fuel truck, Catering truck, Lavatory truck, GPU, ACU
:ann ACU shall be attached last. Time limit 25 minutes — chat-disabled trainees may skip GPU for −3 minutes (tell me if that's you). You may begin. Good luck!
Wingwalking (Final Stage)
Roles by trainee count: 1 → FO or CPT (your call) · 2 → FO + CPT · 3 → Front + FO + CPT · 4 → 2 rounds, split 2:2.
Standard flight path (A) is mandatory.
Keybinds: 5 inside turn · 10 straight pushback · 16 normal walking · 26 outside turn — wait for all trainees to confirm before starting.
Dismiss after wingwalking finishes → go to Marking tab below.

FMC
Simulate a landing aircraft the trainee must lead via FMC to the correct stand/gate (your choice, but communicate it clearly). Insert an aircraft at the start of the landing runway per trainee. On start, 5-second headstart simulating touchdown speed. FMC usually spawns in the air — put it down and confirm the trainee can drive it before starting. All trainees must finish FMC before moving on.
:ann We will begin the FMC simulation. Lead the landing aircraft to the correct gate once called. The aircraft gets a 5-second headstart — start only after the countdown ends.
:cd 5
Attaching
Vehicles: Baggage truck (+ baggage cart), Baggage loader, Glasscover airstairs [L1], Airsteps [L2].
Time limit: 15 minutes. Chat disabled → exempt from baggage truck (doc notes −5 min in the rule, −3 min in the announcement script — use your judgment / confirm with GCL which stands), still required to attach the baggage loader. Fails to finish in time → fail immediately.
Buses
Shuttle bus: at the departure airport, insert an aircraft (preferably A318/A321) on a remote stand (or use mesh planes if available; if no remote stand, park on the tarmac at a realistic distance). Trainees insert one bus each, drive remote stand → gate → back. Check anchored + seatlock off before boarding; check seatlock enabled during the ride (split checks across trainees on multi-trainee rides if needed).

Irizar bus: at the destination airport, trainees insert and park the bus, drive airport → resort (or a reasonable substitute distance if no resort). Driver may not leave the seat while anchored or the vehicle breaks. Check anchored + seatlock both ways.
Dismiss after Irizar bus finishes → go to Marking tab below.
Module 4 — In-Flight Supervision
Final stage of GC training. Notify the host and get permission before supervising — alliance/private (and similar) flight types automatically prohibit M4s.

If allocated as supervisor, announce it in #upcoming-sv (personnel server) in the standard format.

During the flight, assess overall Ground Crew performance and general flight knowledge (communication, admin command usage, professionalism, etc).

After the flight: /gct_mark_m4 to mark overall performance. You DM all generated formats/certificates to the trainee(s) yourself — unless marking is put to SGM review, in which case that responsibility shifts to them. The command auto-logs everything.
When logging & marking M4s, do it in ONE command. Separate all trainees with a comma: /gct_mark_m4 trainee_usernames: User1, User2, User3

It is REQUIRED to send the Graduation Certificate to M4 passers — it's provided below the bot's message and is only visible to you.
M3 & M4 Marking
To pass a role, the trainee must complete the key step and earn a set percentage of marks for that role.
Fail 1 role → your discretion whether the session passes or fails.
Fail 2+ roles → automatic session fail.
Mark via /gct_mark_m2 and /gct_mark_m4.
Misbehaving Trainee
  1. Take evidence (screenshots, videos)
  2. Dismiss the trainee from the training. If it happens mid-flight, have the Flight Host dismiss them instead.
  3. Report to HRD in the Pacific Academy.
Suspected AI Use
Report to HRD in the Pacific Academy — you are not allowed to fail the trainee yourself for suspected AI use; HRD will instruct next steps.

Signs (usually several together, not just one):
  • Answers that make sense in real life but not in SPA's context
  • Overall polished phrasing
  • Repetitive sentence structure across answers
  • Flawless, formal grammar with no personality
Perfect grammar alone isn't proof — treat it as one signal among several.
Ticket Handling — Etiquette
  1. Don't step into tickets claimed by someone else without their permission
  2. Write correctly, stay professional
  3. Don't debate the ticket in the ticket channel — use trainer channels if needed
  4. Don't overping roles — max one role-ping per 24h unless truly necessary
  5. Don't claim tickets you can't actually answer
