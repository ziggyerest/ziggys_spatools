Pre-Flight Duties

Join at xx:30, get teleported and assigned as Deck Manager. Name yourself and equip the Supervisor uniform, then prepare the briefing sheet during xx:30-33 using /briefing in the Briefing Sheet Generator channel or the Announcement Generator.
click to copy
:name me (Senior) Deck Manager
Only post the briefing sheet once all FD members have shown up or told the Deck Manager/host they'll be late or absent. If there's no backup CPT/FO, put yourself in that spot. Airport Roam, Captain Announcements, Second Officer Announcements and First Officer Chat Roleplay should all default to Yes unless a special situation or missing role says otherwise.

Delag to Boarding

FO and SO complete the walkaround and roam with the Deck Manager before economy check-in. The Second Officer runs ATC scripts correctly — without a SO, the FO or Deck Manager takes over ATC duties.
At xx:05-xx:06, all FD except CPT join the CC walk. When the Cabin Manager sends the “[CABIN CREW & FD WALK]” notification, teleport to them as soon as possible and tell FO and SO to do the same.
click to copy
:give me cabin
Wait for the Cabin Manager's green light to board. If the cockpit only has 2 seats, take a spare jumpseat only after all Cabin Crew have one, or sit at the back of Economy. Stay in the cockpit during boarding, then move back into the cabin once Economy boarding finishes.

Pushback to Landing

Watch every FD member throughout the flight and give timely reminders when something's off — for example, if the Captain's AOA is excessive, tell them directly in team chat, private chat, or with :npm admins, and make sure they acknowledge it and fix it.
Don't micromanage FD at every stage unless they genuinely don't know what to do, and never threaten or warn your crew for poor performance — keep the environment positive, and open an FD report in the Personnel Server after the flight if someone's skills are questionable. Stay in your seat from pushback to deboard unless there's an emergency.

Shutdown to Deboard

After the host gives deboard permission, stay seated until all passengers have deboarded, then give your FD members deboard permission with the script below. Lead the FD crew off the aircraft and join post-flight activities if you'd like.
During debrief, tell every FD member about any mistakes and how to fix them, as well as what they did well.

Post-Flight Duties

File the DM Post-Flight Report within 24 hours — this is key quality control for the department. Mention anything that happened, positive or negative, and DM an FDL member if the report needs special attention. If an FD member's skills were questionable, open an FD report with evidence in #help on the Personnel Server.

Handling Situations

Use common sense first. Never discourage an FD member or say anything that could. For a CPT/FO/SO replacement: stay calm, ask the host for permission except in a genuine emergency (switch immediately and inform the host after), and time the switch for cruising unless urgency demands otherwise.
Avoid switching while the aircraft is unanchored or uncruised, while FD members are unaware of the switch, or after 2+ switches already — only break this rule if there's truly no other way.
Switch with whoever holds the necessary certification and needs the least time to switch — FO first for a CPT swap, then yourself if FO can't take it. Document everything in the post-flight report and open FD reports when needed. DM an FDL member if you're ever unsure.
