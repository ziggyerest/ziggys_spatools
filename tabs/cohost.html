Co-Host Overview
Your main duties: answer passenger questions, engage with passengers, assist with upgrades, and make announcements. Stay near Customer Assistance desks from start to taxi.
📍 Positioning
Stay near Customer Assistance desks in the check-in area, or roam the airport. Engage with passengers throughout. Never ignore a question — if you can't respond directly, ask another staff member or :pm the answer.

❓
Answering Passenger Questions
Remain patient — these may be old questions but they are new passengers. Be fun, warm and welcoming. Never ignore a question.
📬 PM Format
Answer via PM
:pm [USER] "[QUESTION]" - To answer your question, [ANSWER]
Example
:pm baconhair "How do I give my order?" - To answer your question, you will be asked in PMs regarding your order when we are at cruising altitude!
🗣️ Common Questions
Economy check-in time
Economy Check-in will open at [TIME]!
What does XX:00 mean
XX:00 displays the minute of time — it just means the minute of any specific hour! For me the time would be [TIME].
No seats left
It says there are zero seats left because the class is not open yet — it will open at [TIME]!
Can't check in
I apologize for that, one moment while I set you up!
Language barrier
Please hold on while I find a staff member who speaks your language!
Where is the gate
The gate is located at [GATE LOCATION] — please follow the signs or feel free to follow me!
Can't sit on plane
Please check you have the correct boarding pass for your class. Some seats are reserved for other classes. Try removing your boarding pass and sitting again!
How to book / pay
There is no booking required — just head to check-in when it opens and check in for free!
⚠️ Issues
Passenger failed to upgrade: Ask the host to open more premium tickets in Self Check-In, then ask them to check in again. Or manually give them their class via commands.

Can't find their seat: Check their class. Some seats require specific boarding passes. Passengers can't sit in some planes if they have their boarding pass equipped.

Language barrier: Try to find a staff member who speaks their language. Use Google Translate as a last resort.

Never upgrade for free unless they meet one of the criteria below. Always verify before upgrading. CC Customer Service Assistants handle most upgrades — only step in when needed.

Check #item-logs in the main server. Find their embed. If not there, ask them to redeem and wait a minute. Do NOT upgrade anyone who claims to have already redeemed without verifying.
